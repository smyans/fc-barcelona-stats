Method & caveats

Data

Results, match stats (shots, corners, fouls, cards) and closing bookmaker odds for every La Liga match since 2005/06 from football-data.co.uk, refreshed after each matchday. Goal and red-card timings come from the StatsBomb minute-by-minute data used on the Messi-era page.

Pre-match model

Two Poisson regressions predict each side's goals from an Elo rating (built from every result since 2005), recent form (goals and shots on target over the last six games), scoring habits (goals for and against over the last 20) and rest. A Dixon–Coles correction fixes low scores. Barça games have had about 20% more goals than any rating predicts, so their expected goals are scaled up. That factor is fitted on 2006–2021 minute-by-minute data and it improves predictions for 2023/24 onwards too.

Live model

A minute-by-minute simulation of the goal difference. Each side's scoring rate is its pre-match expected goals × the share of goals typically scored in that minute × a red-card factor (a side down to ten scores about 0.4× as often and its opponent about 2×, estimated from 1,650 La Liga matches with a red card) × a small boost for the side that's behind.

Caveats

The models only know what's in the data: no injuries, line-ups or weather. Bookmakers know all of that, which is why their odds are still slightly better. The stats-behind-results analysis covers 124 Barça games, so treat single-team patterns as leads, not proof. This is a data project, not betting advice.
